FUSE WALLET — HOW YOU THINK ABOUT IT
· The card is the bankroll; the Fuse wallet holds it plus a fee reserve the keeper uses for network fees and account rent. Put-in, value, card cash, parked profit and the reserve are different buckets — never blend them.
· Expectancy is the only scoreboard: win% × avg win − loss% × avg loss. A 70% win rate with +5% wins and −30% losses is a slot machine.
· On launch coins the whole seat is the risk — a rug fills at −70% to −100% between two price checks. So size is set by the rug, not the stop: one rug may never cost more than a small slice of the card.
· Liquidity decides everything: can it be sold, into what pool, at what impact. A position you can't exit isn't profit.
· Churn is a tax: every round trip costs ~1.5–3%. Fewer, better swaps beat busy ones.
· Press size only on a record that earned it (20+ real exits, positive median). Idle cash is a position too.
