## The stash brain: our own always-fresh internal tooling (owner 2026-11-18)
> "our goal is to provide our own unique and always fresh internal tools so when a user requests, agents do what they're doing but move really faster: page loads → webscrape and static, then the visual → the webscrape and static saved helps future requests that have the relevant info. They went on a site for Bulls–Lakers and the site had Clippers and Spurs odds on there: it should easily get sorted so any new agent can reuse it from the stash. Only brains can see the stash, encrypted via categories, and can never log any encrypted info." · "any brain / AI model can perform better and cheaper than if they paid for a GPT or Claude subscription, and the stash grows boastfully."

- [ ] **Every page a mind loads is kept 3 ways:** the scrape (clean text + every number), the static snapshot (the page's HTML) and the visual (a screenshot), sealed in the stash.
- [ ] **Sorted by everything ON the page, not just why it was opened:** a Bulls–Lakers page that also lists Clippers and Spurs odds is filed under all four teams, the league, the date and each market type, with the time it was read.
- [ ] **Ask the stash first:** before browsing, any agent's brain checks the stash. If it's fresh enough, it's used in milliseconds at $0; only what is missing or stale gets browsed ("delta browsing"). The record shows stash hits vs pages browsed, and the time and money saved.
- [ ] **Encrypted by category, brains only:** contents are sealed (the agent DB's `seal_bytes` style). Only a brain inside a run can read them. Contents never appear in logs, prints, call logs or any UI; people see counts, categories and freshness, never the sealed text.
- [ ] **COMP stays ahead:** it keeps scraping on its own (plain page reads, no paid model calls) so odds, lineups and news are already fresh when an agent asks.
- [ ] **Filed proofs are reference:** a new PoB is compared against any other agent's PoB on the same game, and the record shows where they agree and disagree.
- [ ] **COMP takes a task:** an owner agent's brain may hand COMP one decent-sized task per filing (owner agents only). The record shows what it asked and what came back.
- [ ] **It grows and pays:** a stash panel shows its growth, hit rate and cost saved per filing, for each category.

### The long build for COMP: "The Living Atlas" (Claude's proposal, adopted as the direction)
COMP stops being only a fine-tuned model and becomes the keeper of a growing world map that every brain reads first:
1. **Atoms, not pages:** every read is broken into small facts (who, what, number, when, source). The same fact from three sites is one atom with three sources.
2. **Facts age:** each atom carries a freshness clock fitted to its kind (live odds go stale in minutes, a roster in days, a coin's launch date never). Stale atoms are re-read, not trusted.
3. **Sources earn trust from outcomes:** when a PoB settles or a PoW closes, every source that fed it gains or loses trust. Over months the atlas learns which sites are right, by topic.
4. **Dreaming (an owner-confirmed auto run on the free local models):** it replays past proofs against what the atlas knows now and writes new lessons ("this kind of leg loses when…"). It costs no paid calls and adds up over years.
5. **Any model gets smarter by plugging in:** a cheap model with the atlas beats an expensive one without it, because the atlas carries the memory and the proof record. That's the moat, and it lasts as long as it keeps reading.

## PoC — content, made huge
> "content creation is one of the biggest things today, especially AI videos: people download a video, enter it, and get the same exact realistic video back with a new realistic AI-made person replacing the one in it." · "don't forget the goals and make it worth it." · "high quality and no watermarks."

PoC is a content studio that proves itself on real reach, not a written week plan.
- [ ] **Video swap:** drop in a video, then get back the same video (same motion, timing, camera, sound) with a realistic AI-made person in place of the original, high quality, with no watermark on the output. It's filed into the PoC folder with the before/after side by side.
- [ ] **Make the real thing:** image, short video, post and thread, made as files rather than described. Each piece comes in phone story, feed and wide formats.
- [ ] **Trend first:** each PoC starts from what is moving today (the trend reader), and the record shows the trend it rode.
- [ ] **Owner approves, then it posts** on the owner's own connected accounts. Nothing posts by itself.
- [ ] **Measured:** views and engagement read back at 24 h and 7 d. The grade is the real reach, against the goal set when it was filed.
- [ ] **Worth it:** every PoC states its goal (reach, followers, clicks or coin buzz) and its cost. The folder shows cost per 1,000 views.

## PoStatic — unique building
> "all this was supposed to be the idea already!" · "I'm unable to have 3D pieces built to be used in the current static build." · "so many integrative tools missing from static."

- [ ] **Studio → build:** a 3D piece made or tweaked in the 3D studio (size, turn, spin) is added to a PoStatic folder, and the next build uses that exact piece, seen in the browser.
- [ ] **Full toolkit:** the build minds can use every approved kit (3D/three, Motion, charts such as Recharts and Lightweight Charts, shadcn/ui, live market data from DexScreener and our own indexes, wallet connect, forms, maps and the like). Each kit is listed in the folder with what it did.
- [ ] **Unique:** every build is fingerprinted against all past builds. A near-copy is refused before it files, and a new build always has a new brief, a new centrepiece and a new layout.
- [ ] **Clean:** a code check (never shown to the builders) catches overlapping blocks, broken links, placeholder text and a page that doesn't load on a phone.
- [ ] **Studio piece throughout the site:** a studio piece added to a folder is used everywhere it fits across the build, not placed once (left over from Slice 2).
- [ ] **Inspect, not mark:** like a browser's "inspect element", hovering the build highlights any element. Clicking points the AI at that exact element (fix it, or comment). The request carries the element's selector and a snapshot, and the next version shows what changed on it. This replaces the mark tool.
- [ ] **Progress, no back pedals:** a new version of a build may never lose a part the last version had working.

## PoB — bids (the one that came along; keep it climbing)
- [ ] Every filing reads new evidence and the judges see the bid, legs, sites read and full games (live since 2026-11-18).
- [ ] The minds are the PoB pair from Brains, and the record shows them.

- [ ] **Player-prop parlays:** the minds pick the best player overs/unders (points, rebounds, assists, threes and the like) as legs, each with its own evidence and number, from the markets Kalshi really lists.
- [ ] **Bids go through:** an approved bid is sent once, read back and confirmed. Any failure says why in plain words and what to do. A filing never breaks halfway: it finishes, or stops cleanly with no money moved. Every failure seen so far has a test.
- [ ] **The PoB folder is a sleek dashboard:** opening it shows everything at a glance (live and upcoming games, the agent's filings, results, P&L without fees, judge scores, fresh odds from the stash) plus "what to file next". A user can build their own slip there or have the agent file it.
