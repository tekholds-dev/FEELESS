FEELESS — SUPREME INTELLIGENCE UPGRADE
Audit the latest FEELESS codebase, including Agents, PoW, Colony, Honey Pot, wallets, desktop, FEE CAT, model infrastructure, archives, trading systems and browser research infrastructure.
Build on what exists. Upgrade weak mechanics. Consolidate duplicates. Don't reinvent working systems.
The objective is a professional, autonomous, self-improving intelligence ecosystem—not simply more AI agents, more research steps or more browser activity.
1. PoW — Faster, Deeper, More Intelligent Research
Preserve the existing independent two-mind, four-coin PoW architecture.
Upgrade it with:
* Adaptive investigation: Research depth determined by uncertainty, evidence quality, risk and information gained—not rigid step counts.
* Multi-fact extraction: Capture all relevant, verifiable findings from a page in one structured evidence snapshot, rather than saving only one or two facts.
* Intelligent source rotation: Rank sources by reliability, novelty, cost, freshness and historical contribution.
* Research deduplication: Reuse verified factual evidence without sharing the minds' independent conclusions.
* Evidence lineage: Record each claim's source, timestamp, supporting data and contradictions.
* Research efficiency: Measure useful information per dollar, latency, page visit and decision impact.
* Continuous monitoring: Reevaluate the original trade thesis as new evidence arrives.
Real Browser Research — Critical Upgrade
The agents must perform genuine, observable browser investigations with realistic cursor movement, scrolling, clicking, navigation and page inspection.
Speed objective: Reduce the current approximately 5–10-minute investigation cycle without sacrificing research quality, evidence completeness or source diversity.
* Eliminate unnecessary waits, repeated page loads, duplicated research and inefficient browser actions.
* Use event-driven page readiness instead of arbitrary long delays.
* Let each useful page remain visibly inspectable for approximately 10 seconds when practical, without forcing the backend to idle unnecessarily.
* Capture substantial structured evidence from a successfully loaded page: token identity, liquidity, holders, transactions, concentration, contract risks, creator history, trading activity and other available relevant information.
* Preserve page provenance, screenshots where useful, timestamps, extracted facts and evidence confidence.
* Do not mark an investigation complete merely because a page was visited.
* Treat Solscan, Pump.fun and other critical sources as required verification targets where applicable. If inaccessible, record the gap and seek independent corroboration rather than pretending verification succeeded.
* Avoid revisiting pages unless freshness or missing evidence justifies it.
Bot Checks, Sessions & Cookies
Implement a legitimate human-assisted browser recovery workflow.
When a page presents a CAPTCHA, bot challenge or authentication barrier:
1. Detect the challenge and pause that site's investigation.
2. Show the user the actual browser session with a clear 45-second intervention window.
3. Allow the user to complete the challenge manually in the normal browser interface.
4. Resume automatically when access is legitimately restored.
5. Persist refreshed session cookies securely where the site's terms and authentication requirements permit.
6. Replace expired session state safely rather than accumulating stale cookies.
7. If unresolved, record the source as temporarily unavailable and continue independent research.
Support authorized session reuse between compatible browser workers operating under the same account and security boundary. Do not expose raw cookies to AI prompts, logs or other users.
Do not automate CAPTCHA solving, impersonate human interaction to defeat access controls, or bypass a site's restrictions. Genuine browser automation and authorized manual intervention are the goal.
Smooth Live Browser Screens
The agent browser experience must be watchable and responsive.
* Target smooth 30 FPS live viewing where hardware and network conditions permit.
* Keep cursor, scrolling and navigation visually synchronized with real browser activity.
* Prevent UI polling, screenshots and video rendering from blocking research execution.
* Avoid repeated full-page transfers when incremental updates are sufficient.
* Optimize streaming, rendering, frame scheduling and browser resource usage.
* Allow multiple investigations without excessive CPU, memory or network contention.
* Clearly distinguish live browser activity from screenshots or replayed frames.
* Profile actual bottlenecks before changing browser architecture.
Success standard: Faster investigations, richer evidence per visit, fewer repeated challenges, and smooth observable browser sessions—not artificial speed from skipping research.
2. PoI — Persistent Intelligence Evolution
Create a real Proof of Intelligence (PoI) subsystem integrated with PoW, Agents and Colony.
Implement:
* Private, encrypted, signed and versioned Research DNA.
* Unique research fingerprints for each mind.
* Source reputation and intelligent research rotation.
* Cross-brain inheritance of validated research techniques.
* Controlled mutation, combination and retirement of research methods.
* Shadow trades and counterfactual evaluations.
* Evidence lineage, deduplication and research efficiency scoring.
* Historical and out-of-sample evaluation.
* Automatic promotion, rejection and rollback under deterministic rules.
* Permanent generation history.
PoI must learn which browser investigation methods produce the most useful evidence, not merely which trades happened to win.
3. Hardcoded Evolution Kernel
Build a deterministic, model-independent engine responsible for:
* Evidence validation and freshness.
* Research budgets and adaptive resource allocation.
* Browser source scheduling and retry policies.
* Fingerprint versioning and integrity.
* Evaluation and promotion thresholds.
* Risk limits and execution boundaries.
* Regression detection and rollback.
* Auditability and failure recovery.
Hardcoded means enforceable rules—not frozen intelligence.
Rules can evolve through versioned, tested and approved releases. AI models may propose improvements but cannot silently override live safeguards.
4. Model-Independent Memory
Intelligence must survive replacing or upgrading AI models.
Support:
* Keeping existing validated intelligence.
* Starting fresh candidate intelligence.
* Model-assisted memory migration.
* Comparing old and new strategies before adoption.
* Preserving original evidence and research history.
Keep canonical memory independent of model-specific context.
5. Colony — Autonomous Economic Coordination
Keep Colony model-free.
Strengthen existing colonization, continuous PoW, Honey Pot, agent wallets, contribution percentages and rewards.
Verify or implement:
* Explicit user-controlled X% contributions from realized net winnings.
* Accurate fee-adjusted profit accounting.
* Verified on-chain settlements.
* Idempotent contribution processing.
* Treasury and reward reconciliation.
* Separation of earned, owed, transferred and distributed funds.

PoI may inform Colony reputation and research allocation but cannot independently move treasury funds.
