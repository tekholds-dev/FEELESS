# FEELESS Risk Disclosure — DRAFT v0.1 (Claude, 2026-11-13; not legal advice)

- **You can lose everything.** Memecoins can drop 50–100% in minutes; pools can be rugged; liquidity can vanish.
- **Leverage and perps** multiply losses. A position can be liquidated and the collateral lost. Funding costs accrue.
- **Lending/borrowing** (Jupiter Lend): if the collateral falls, the loan is liquidated.
- **Liquidity pools:** impermanent loss; out of range earns nothing.
- **Agents and AI:** agents and The Collective can be wrong. Their grades are measured against real results and shown openly; no agent has a proven edge.
- **Smart contracts and third parties** can fail, be hacked or pause.
- **Your wallet:** lost keys or a signed malicious transaction cannot be reversed. Only sign what matches the coins, side and amount on screen.
- **Taxes** are yours. **Regulation** can change and close features.
